What it costs from Charlotte

My pricing is the same everywhere: performances from $500 and wedding packages from $500 to $1,000. The first hour of round-trip travel is free and the rest is $100 per hour, so most Charlotte-area events add about $150 to $250 for travel. I will send you a detailed invoice breaking down everything including any travel fees.
