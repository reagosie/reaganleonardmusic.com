Meet Reagan

A Greenville musician who has played 300+ events, and treats yours like the only one

I'm Reagan Leonard, a singer-guitarist born and raised in Spartanburg, SC and based in Greer. For 15 years I've played weddings, company events and parties across the Carolinas, Georgia and Tennessee: mostly acoustic covers of pop, folk, country and rock from the 60s to today, from Jack Johnson and The Lumineers to John Mayer, Chris Stapleton and The Beatles.

I bring my own sound system, learn the songs that matter to you, and show up early. You get one vendor, one contact, and a room that sounds like a full band without one.
