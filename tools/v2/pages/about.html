About

Reagan Leonard

Singer-guitarist. Born and raised in Spartanburg, based in Greer, playing weddings and events across the Southeast for 15 years.

The short version

One musician, a full sound, and a set list that fits the room

I've been playing guitar and singing for people since my teens, and doing it for a living for 15 years. In that time I've played more than 300 weddings, corporate events and private parties across South Carolina, North Carolina, Georgia and Tennessee.

What I do is simple to describe: acoustic covers of songs people love, from the 60s to today, played live with a voice that suits a ceremony as well as a party. What clients notice is the rest of it: that I answer emails quickly, learn the songs that matter to them, show up early with everything I need, and read the room.

I play one event per day. When you book a date, it's yours, and you get my full attention for it.

How I work

What you can count on

A real reply, fast

You write to me, not an agency. I reply personally, usually within a day, with a real number rather than a range.

Your songs, prepared

A free planning call, a set list built around your requests, and new songs learned with notice. Couples get a preview of their first dance if they want one.

Nothing for you to manage

I bring the sound system, microphone and cables, arrive early, and pack down quietly. Your venue needs one outlet.

The music and the gear

What it sounds like

Mostly acoustic pop, folk, country and rock: Jack Johnson, The Lumineers, Ed Sheeran, John Mayer, Chris Stapleton, Johnny Cash, The Beatles, Van Morrison, Sinatra, plus a wedding set, a worship set and a Christmas set. The song list has all {{song_count}} songs.
