Where I play

Based in Greer, SC. Playing within about 250 miles.

The first hour of round-trip travel is free; after that it's $100 per hour, itemized on your quote. Here is what that means for the places I play most.

Greenville and Upstate South Carolina

Home · no travel fee

Greenville, Greer, Spartanburg, Anderson, Clemson, Easley, Travelers Rest, Simpsonville, Fountain Inn, Seneca and everywhere in between. Most of my weddings and parties are here.

Asheville and Western North Carolina

About 1 to 2 hours · travel about $50 to $200

Asheville, Hendersonville, Flat Rock, Brevard, Saluda, Tryon, Lake Lure, Highlands, Cashiers, Waynesville and the High Country around Boone and Blowing Rock.

Weddings and events in Asheville

Charlotte, NC

About 90 minutes · travel about $150 to $250

Uptown and SouthPark, Lake Norman, Davidson, Huntersville, Matthews, Fort Mill and Rock Hill.

Weddings and events in Charlotte

North Georgia

About 1.5 to 3 hours · travel about $100 to $400

Athens, Gainesville, Toccoa, Clayton, Helen, Blue Ridge, Dahlonega, Lake Lanier and the north side of Atlanta.

East Tennessee

About 2.5 to 3.5 hours · travel about $300 to $500

Knoxville, Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Sevierville, Johnson City and Chattanooga. I've played rehearsal dinners and weddings in Tennessee for families who found me online; the drive is not a problem.

Elsewhere in SC

Columbia about 1.5 hours · Charleston about 3.5 hours

Columbia, Rock Hill, Aiken and the Midlands are easy. Charleston and the coast are at the edge of the radius; ask, and if the date works I'll quote it.

Out-of-town clients

Booking from far away is normal

Many of my clients live out of state and are planning a wedding or rehearsal dinner back home in the Carolinas. The process is the same: the form, a quote, a planning call, and I take care of the rest. I bring my own sound, so the venue only needs an outlet.

Destination weddings in the mountains, on a farm or at a winery are a large part of my calendar. I know the drives, the venues and how long setup takes on a hillside.
