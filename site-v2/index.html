Meet Reagan

A Greenville musician who has played 300+ events, and treats yours like the only one

I'm Reagan Leonard, a singer-guitarist born and raised in Spartanburg, SC and based in Greer. For 15 years I've played weddings, company events and parties across the Carolinas, Georgia and Tennessee: mostly acoustic covers of pop, folk, country and rock from the 60s to today, from Jack Johnson and The Lumineers to John Mayer, Chris Stapleton and The Beatles.

I bring my own sound system, learn the songs that matter to you, and show up early. You get one vendor, one contact, and a room that sounds like a full band without one.

Good to know

Quick answers

How much does it cost to book Reagan Leonard Music?

Performances start at $500. Wedding packages run $500 to $1,000, and most events land between $500 and $3,000 depending on the length of the performance and travel. The full rates are on the pricing page, and every quote is itemized before you commit.

Can you play our song?

Almost certainly. The current song list has 248 songs across ten genres, and with enough notice before your date I will learn songs on request, including first-dance and ceremony songs.

Do you bring your own sound equipment?

Yes. All I need is a power outlet or an extension cord nearby. My setup is a Bose L1 Compact sound system, a microphone and stand, a Boss RC-30 looper on request, and all the cables, and it fills indoor and outdoor venues of up to 500 guests. I play a Takamine acoustic guitar.

Is it just you, or a band?

Just me: one singer-guitarist with a full sound system. That means one vendor to coordinate, a small footprint at the venue, and a price well below a band's, with a volume that guests can still talk over during dinner and cocktails.
