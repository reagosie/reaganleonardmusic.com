Up the mountain from Greenville

About 75 minutes from Greer, with everything in the car

Asheville is a regular drive for me: up I-26 through Saluda and Hendersonville, or across to Brevard, Cashiers and Highlands. Mountain venues are where a solo acoustic act makes the most sense. There is no stage to build and no band to feed; I set up on a lawn, a barn floor or a terrace with one outlet and play at a level that carries without shouting over the view.

Outdoor ceremonies

Most of my ceremonies are outside. My Bose L1 Compact system is built for it: clear across a hillside for 200 guests, and still gentle for the front row. If your ceremony site has no power, tell me and we'll plan around it.

What it costs from Asheville

Same pricing as everywhere: from $500, wedding packages $500 to $1,000. The first hour of round-trip travel is free and the rest is $100 per hour, so most Asheville-area events add about $100 to $150; Highlands and Boone add a little more. Your quote shows the exact figure.

Questions

Booking a mountain wedding

Can you play our song?

Almost certainly. The current song list has 248 songs across ten genres, and with enough notice before your date I will learn songs on request, including first-dance and ceremony songs. This list is always growing and if you have a special request, just ask me. I can probably learn it but if I don't think I can do it justice, I will be honest and straightforward with you about that.

How far will you travel?

I'm based in Greer, SC and play anywhere within about 250 miles: Upstate South Carolina, Western North Carolina, Charlotte, North Georgia and East Tennessee. The first hour of round-trip travel is free; after that travel is $100 per hour.

Do you play outdoor ceremonies?

Yes, and most of my wedding ceremonies are outdoors. The sound system carries across a lawn or garden without being loud up front. I only need power within reach of an extension cord.

What does ceremony music include?

Up to 30 minutes of soft instrumental guitar while guests are seated, then your processional, any songs during the ceremony itself, and the recessional. You choose the songs; I will suggest options if you would like help.

Planning a wedding in the mountains?

Send the date and venue and I'll confirm availability with the travel already worked out.
