If the pricing page doesn't answer your question or if you'd like to receive a custom quote for your event, please contact me via the contact form at the bottom of this page!

I play all sorts of events and am open to just about anything. However, I specialize in weddings, corporate events, and private parties.

If you're wondering whether I'll perform at your event...as long as I'm available and you have a healthy entertainment budget, the answer is almost certainly yes!

I can play just about anything if given enough advance notice! My current repertoire is shown on the Song List page. This list is always growing and if you have a special request, just ask me. I can probably learn it but if I don't think I can do it justice, I will be honest and straightforward with you about that.

Yes! One of the best parts about hiring me for your event is that all I need is an outlet/extension cord nearby; I take care of the rest. My full setup provides a huge amount of sound that will rock any venue, indoor or outdoor, up to 500 people!

My full setup includes: 1 Bose L1 Compact System, a mic and mic stand, a Boss RC-30 looper (upon request), and all the necessary cables. I perform with a Takamine acoustic guitar.
