How your wedding day goes with Reagan

Pick the parts of the day you want live music for. Every package includes my own sound system and a microphone for toasts, so all your venue needs is an outlet.

Ceremony

Soft instrumental guitar for up to 30 minutes while guests arrive, then your processional, the songs you choose for the ceremony itself, and the recessional. Included in every package.

Cocktail hour

Up to an hour of acoustic favorites, from Jack Johnson and John Mayer to Sinatra and The Beatles, at a level people can still talk over. Gold and Diamond packages.

Reception

Up to two hours of live music for dinner and dancing, with your first dance and requests worked in. Diamond package. Rehearsal dinners are available on their own.

Prices above are for events within an hour's round trip of Greer, SC. Travel beyond that is $100 per hour; see the pricing page. Not sure which package fits? Tell me about your day and I'll help you choose.

Common questions

Do you bring your own sound equipment?

Yes. All I need is an outlet or extension cord nearby. My setup (a Bose L1 Compact system, microphone and stand, and a looper on request) fills indoor and outdoor venues for up to 500 guests.

Can you play our song?

Almost certainly. My current list is on the song list page, and with enough notice before your date I will learn songs on request. This list is always growing and if you have a special request, just ask me. I can probably learn it but if I don't think I can do it justice, I will be honest and straightforward with you about that.

How far will you travel?

I'm based in Greer, SC and play anywhere within about 250 miles: Upstate South Carolina, Western North Carolina, Charlotte, North Georgia and East Tennessee. The first hour of round-trip travel is free; after that it's $100 per hour.

How do we book you?

Send the availability form with your date and venue. I'll reply with a quote, we'll have a quick call to plan your songs, and a signed agreement holds the date.
